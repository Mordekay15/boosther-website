Why BoostHer

Every founder starts with a question: where do I begin?

Too many brilliant ideas stay in notebooks. Not because they aren’t good, but because the people behind them were never shown the path. BoostHer is a student-driven community in Turku that makes entrepreneurship approachable, practical and genuinely fun.

We bring people together around real conversations, hands-on workshops and founders who are honest about the journey — so you can stop wondering and start building.

  1. 01

    Knowledge

    Startup basics without the jargon: validating ideas, finding customers, pricing, pitching and the terms everyone pretends to know.

  2. 02

    Courage

    A room full of people cheering you on. Share half-finished ideas, get feedback and take the first step with others beside you.

  3. 03

    Mindset

    Think bigger, act consistently. We build the habits and the confidence that turn “someday” into “today.”

What we do

Ways to get boosted

From your very first idea to your first customer, there’s a place for you here.

Workshops

Practical, hands-on sessions where you work on your own idea: from defining your target market to building your first pitch.

Signature event

Female Founder Brunch

Good food, great company and honest stories from founders who’ve been there. Our favourite way to connect, ask anything and leave inspired.

Founder talks

Entrepreneurs, investors and startup people share what really happens behind the scenes, including the messy, unglamorous parts.

A day in the life

Follow our “My Day” series to see how founders and our team actually spend their days building something of their own.

Learn the lingo

Bite-sized facts, terms and how-tos so that MVP, bootstrapping and pre-seed never feel intimidating again.

Community & network

Meet co-founders, mentors and friends across Turku’s universities and startup scene. Your future team might be at the next event.

Our method

Five steps from idea to business

A simple framework we keep coming back to in our workshops. Work through it once and you’ll know more about your idea than most people ever do.

Try it at a workshop
  1. 1

    People

    Identify your target market.

  2. 2

    Pain

    Understand their struggle.

  3. 3

    Promise

    Create their solution.

  4. 4

    Process

    Be their guide.

  5. 5

    Price

    Price based on the value you bring.
