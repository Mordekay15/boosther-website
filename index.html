About

BoostHer is a concept under Boost Turku, promoting the participation of women and minorities in the startup ecosystem through events, workshops and networking opportunities. We provide practical tools for entrepreneurship and lower the threshold for joining the startup community.

Mission

Our mission is to increase the representation of women and minorities in entrepreneurship and startup ecosystem.

Vision

A diverse and inclusive startup ecosystem where everyone has opportunity to innovate and succeed.
